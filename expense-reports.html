Expense reports

Where did my money go? Reports that answer it.

A good expense report answers real questions: how much, on what, compared with when. FinInsight AI builds them from your own transactions in a second.

Download for Android All features
Free · Android 8.0 or newer · your data stays on your phone

Any period you need

This month, last month, a quarter, a calendar year, the Indian financial year, year-to-date, the last 3, 6 or 12 months, or custom dates. Swipe back through any month or jump with the month picker.

Spending analysis, four ways

  • Category-wise — food, groceries, shopping, travel, bills, EMIs and more
  • Payee and merchant-wise — who you pay most
  • Payment method — UPI, card, net banking, ATM cash, auto-debit
  • Day by day — a calendar of your spending

Comparisons and trends

Month-on-month and year-on-year comparisons show whether you're spending more or less. Statistics show your average monthly spending, the highest and lowest months, and each category's 12-month trend.

Export, share and keep

Export an expense report to PDF or Excel for your records, your accountant or a reimbursement. Or share a report card picture — totals and category names only, never account numbers, payees or messages.

Questions

Can I export expenses to Excel?

Yes — reports export to Excel and PDF.

Can I see a yearly expense report?

Yes, for a calendar year or the Indian financial year (April to March).

Does the report card include my account details?

No. It shows only totals and category names.

Are reports made on a server?

No. Every report is built on your phone.
