Automatic categorisation that understands bank messages

Every bank words its SMS differently. FinInsight's engine reads them all — amount, account, payee, channel (UPI, card, IMPS, NEFT, ATM, NACH) — and categorises each payment automatically. Correct it once and it learns. Tap Why FinInsight thinks so to see the reasoning behind any category.

Ask in plain words

Type the question the way you'd ask a friend: "How much did I spend on food last month?", "only HDFC", "food this year", or "add 250 chai cash". The Ask bar searches and answers from your own data, on your phone.

Insights you'd miss in a spreadsheet

  • Month-end forecast — "On track for ₹46.7K this month"
  • Unusual spending alert — an unusually large payment in a category
  • Charged twice? and price rises on recurring payments
  • Small spends under ₹200 that add up, and weekend spending
  • Category drift — a category creeping up month after month
  • Bills due vs bank balance, family money sent and received, cashback earned

Explainable by design

Every insight and the money score out of 100 shows its evidence — the exact transactions behind the number. No black box, no cloud, no advertising ID.

Questions

Is FinInsight's AI a chatbot?

It's an on-device insight engine with a plain-words Ask bar, built for money questions. It doesn't send your data to a cloud AI service.

Does the AI need internet?

No. It runs on your phone.

Can the AI be wrong?

Sometimes — every insight shows its evidence so you can check, and corrections are remembered.

Is my data used to train an AI?

No. Your data never leaves your phone.
