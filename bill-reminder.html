Bills, EMIs and dues

Bills and EMIs, listed before they're due

A missed bill costs a late fee; a missed EMI can hurt your credit score. FinInsight AI finds upcoming payments in your messages and reminds you in time — no due dates to type in.

What it finds

  • Credit card bills — amount, minimum due and due date
  • Loan EMIs — home loan, car loan and personal loan debits, including NACH and auto-debit mandates
  • Insurance premiums — LIC and other life and health insurance renewals
  • Utility bills — electricity, phone and broadband bills from the biller's message
  • Payment requests — UPI collect requests waiting for you
  • Expected refunds — so you notice when one doesn't arrive

Bills due vs money in the bank

FinInsight compares this month's upcoming bills with your latest bank balance. If the balance looks short before a bill or EMI is due, it tells you — before the auto-debit bounces and the bank charges you.

Recurring bills, tracked over time

Monthly bills and EMIs are recognised as recurring, so you can see the total of your fixed monthly payments, spot a bill that went up, and plan the rest of the month around them. Rent paid by UPI or bank transfer is tracked too.

Reminders that respect your privacy

Reminders are created on your phone from your own messages. Nothing is sent to a server, and there's no account to sign in to.

Questions

Do I need to enter due dates?

No. Bills and EMIs are found in your messages. You can still add a reminder yourself.

Does it pay my bills?

No. FinInsight reminds you; you pay with your bank or UPI app as usual.

Will it warn me about a low balance before an EMI?

Yes, when your bank's balance messages show the balance may not cover upcoming bills.

Does it track rent?

Yes. Rent paid by UPI or bank transfer is categorised as rent and housing.
