Expense tracker

The expense tracker that fills itself in

Most expense trackers fail for one reason: you stop typing in every payment. FinInsight AI records your spending automatically from the bank, card and UPI messages already on your phone — and never uploads them.

Why an expense tracker without manual entry works

A manual expense app needs you to remember every chai, auto ride and online order. After two weeks most people give up, and the totals stop meaning anything. Your bank already sends a message for nearly every payment, so FinInsight starts from those:

  • Every UPI, debit card, credit card and net-banking payment is picked up as the SMS arrives
  • Past months fill in on day one from the messages already on your phone
  • Cash spends take two taps, or type "add 250 chai cash" in the Ask bar
  • Missing months? Import a bank statement (PDF, even password-protected, or CSV) to fill the gaps

Totals you can trust

A simple SMS reader counts the same rupee twice. FinInsight is built to avoid that:

  • Transfers between your own accounts aren't counted as spending
  • Credit-card bill payments aren't counted again — the card purchases already were
  • Refunds are matched to the original purchase
  • Investments such as SIPs and RDs are kept apart from spending
  • Correct a category once and the same payee is categorised that way from then on

Track my spending: category-wise and payee-wise

Spending is grouped into categories such as food, groceries, shopping, travel, bills, rent and EMIs, and you can add your own. Open any category or payee to see every payment, compare this month with last month, or view a day-by-day calendar. Reports cover any month, quarter, year or Indian financial year, and export to PDF or Excel.

Private by design

There is no FinInsight server and no account to create. Messages are read and every total is calculated on your phone, stored encrypted inside the app, and never uploaded. Personal chats and OTPs are skipped. No advertising ID is shared.

Who it's for

Households use it as a home expense tracker for groceries, bills, rent and school fees. Students use it as a simple daily expense tracker for pocket money and UPI spends. Salaried people use it to see where the salary went each month. Whatever you call it — an expense log, an expense diary or an expense book — it keeps itself up to date.

Questions

Is FinInsight a free expense tracker?

Yes. Automatic tracking, this month's and last month's reports, Scam Shield, alerts and app lock are free.

Does it work with every Indian bank?

It reads messages from all major Indian banks, credit cards, UPI apps and wallets, including HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis and Kotak. If a message is misread, correct it once.

Do I need internet or a bank login?

No. FinInsight works from the SMS on your phone. There is no bank login, no account and no upload.

Can I track cash expenses?

Yes — add cash in two taps, or type "add 250 chai cash" in the Ask bar.
