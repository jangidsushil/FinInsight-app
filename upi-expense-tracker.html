UPI expense tracker

Every UPI payment, tracked automatically

UPI makes paying effortless — and keeping count hard. Small payments across several apps add up fast. FinInsight AI tracks them all from your bank's messages, whichever app you paid with.

Download for Android All features
Free · Android 8.0 or newer · your data stays on your phone

Works with every UPI app

Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Amazon Pay, CRED or your bank's own app — the payment is debited from your bank account, and your bank sends a message. FinInsight reads that message, so every UPI payment is tracked in one place, no matter which app you used.

Payees made readable

  • UPI IDs turned into names you recognise, which you can rename once
  • Each payee's history — how much you've paid a shop, a person or a service, month by month
  • Money sent to friends and family kept apart from shopping, with groups and trips for shared costs
  • Transfers to your own accounts recognised, so they aren't counted as spending

Small payments, big picture

Fifty rupees here and two hundred there are easy to forget. FinInsight adds them up by category and payee, shows the month's pace and forecast, and finds regular payments and subscriptions paid through UPI AutoPay.

Scam Shield for UPI fraud messages

Fake KYC updates, electricity cut-off threats, e-challan links, reward-point expiry and payment requests from strangers are common UPI scams. Scam Shield flags these messages on your phone and explains why they look suspicious.

UPI Lite, QR codes and money received

Scan-and-pay QR payments at local shops, money sent to people and money received from them are all recorded, along with UPI Lite top-ups and any UPI Lite payment your bank sends a message for. The month's UPI transactions can be viewed as a report or exported, which is handy when a UPI app's own history only shows one app.

Questions

Do I need to link Google Pay or PhonePe?

No. FinInsight reads your bank's SMS for each UPI payment, so nothing needs to be linked.

Does it read my UPI PIN or OTPs?

No. OTPs and personal messages are skipped and never stored. FinInsight never asks for a UPI PIN.

Are UPI AutoPay subscriptions found?

Yes — regular payments, including AutoPay ones, are recognised from their pattern.

Is my UPI history uploaded?

No. Everything stays on your phone, encrypted. There is no server and no account.
