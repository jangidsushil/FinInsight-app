Privacy

An expense tracker with nothing to leak

Most finance apps ask you to sign up, link your bank, and trust their servers with your data. FinInsight AI takes the privacy-first path: everything happens on your phone, so there's no server to hack or sell from.

A secure, on-device expense tracker: no login, no bank linking

There's no sign-up, no email or phone number to register, and no bank password or account-aggregator consent to give. FinInsight works from the SMS already on your phone — offline, without internet.

Encrypted on the phone, not in the cloud

  • Encrypted storage inside the app — only money messages are kept
  • No cloud backup — Android's automatic backup is switched off for FinInsight
  • Encrypted backup only when you make one, protected with your own password

No advertising ID shared

The advertising-ID and ad-tracking permissions are removed from the app, so your advertising ID can't be read or shared. Nothing from your messages or books is shared with anyone.

Minimal permissions, maximum control

  • Permissions: SMS, notifications and fingerprint — no contacts, location, microphone or camera
  • App lock with PIN or fingerprint
  • Hide amounts with one tap when someone's looking at your screen
  • Report a problem opens your own email, so you see exactly what's sent

This is the spirit of India's data protection law (DPDP Act): collect only what's needed. FinInsight collects nothing at all.

Questions

Does FinInsight work without internet?

Yes. Reading messages and calculating everything happens offline.

Is it safe to give an app SMS permission?

Only to an app you trust with your inbox. FinInsight has no server, so your messages have nowhere to go.

What happens if I lose my phone?

The app's data is encrypted and the app can be locked. Restore on a new phone from an encrypted backup you made.

Does FinInsight show ads?

The free version shows ads, without an advertising ID. Your data is never used for them.
