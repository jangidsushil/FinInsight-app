Scam Shield

Spot a fraud SMS before it costs you

Scam messages copy the look of real bank and government alerts. Scam Shield checks incoming SMS on your phone and tells you, in plain words, why a message looks like fraud.

Scams it recognises

  • KYC or PAN update — banks never ask you to update KYC through an SMS link or call
  • Account, card or SIM block threats
  • Electricity disconnection — "your power will be cut tonight, call this officer"
  • Traffic e-challan links — official challans are only on echallan.parivahan.gov.in
  • Reward points expiring — a link or APK to "redeem" that steals card details
  • Parcel or courier held, income-tax refund and prize or lottery messages
  • Part-time job and task scams — "like videos and earn"
  • Police, CBI or customs threats and digital arrest
  • Aadhaar misuse, gas connection threats and instant loan offers
  • UPI PIN to receive money — a PIN is only ever needed to pay

How it decides

Scam Shield weighs who sent the message (a registered bank header or a personal number), whether it carries a link or an APK, and the lure and urgency in the wording. You see a risk level and the reasons — not just a warning.

How to identify a fraud SMS yourself

  • Real banks don't send links to update KYC, PAN or Aadhaar
  • Never install an APK sent in a message
  • You never need a UPI PIN or OTP to receive money
  • Government agencies don't arrest anyone over a call or SMS
  • Report fraud at 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in, and report spam SMS through Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu)

Checked on your phone

Messages are checked on the device. Nothing is sent to a server to be scanned.

Questions

Is Scam Shield free?

Yes. Scam Shield is part of the free version.

Does it block the message?

It flags the message and explains why. Don't click links or call numbers in it; delete it and report it if you wish.

What should I do if I've already paid a scammer?

Call the cybercrime helpline 1930 immediately and your bank, and file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.

Does it read my personal chats to find scams?

It checks incoming SMS on your phone. Personal messages are never stored.
