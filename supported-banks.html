How bank support works

Each bank sends SMS from registered headers — HDFC Bank from HDFCBK, SBI from SBIINB or SBIUPI, ICICI from ICICIT. FinInsight knows these headers and each bank's wording, and it also reads well-formed messages from banks not on the list. If a message is misread, correct it once and FinInsight remembers.

Questions

My bank isn't listed — will it work?

Usually, yes. Well-formed bank messages are read even from banks not on the list. If something is misread, correct it once.

Does it work with UPI apps?

Yes. UPI payments are read from your bank's SMS, whichever UPI app you used.

Does it work with credit cards?

Yes — card spends, bills and due dates. See the credit card tracker.

Do I need to log in to my bank?

No. FinInsight never asks for bank logins.
