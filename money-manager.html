Money manager

A money manager that does the bookkeeping

Call it a money manager, an expense manager, a kharcha app or a hisab kitab app — the job is the same: know what came in, what went out and what's left. FinInsight AI keeps that account for you, on your phone.

Free · Android 8.0 or newer · your data stays on your phone

Your daily kharcha, without the diary

A paper diary or a cash book app only knows what you write in it. FinInsight reads the money messages your bank already sends, so every UPI, card and bank payment is in the book the moment it happens. Add cash spends in two taps and the hisaab is complete — the monthly kharcha, the ghar kharch and the little spends that usually go missing.

Income and expenses in one place

  • Money in: salary, interest, refunds and cashback
  • Money out: spending by category and payee, with transfers to your own accounts left out
  • Invested: SIPs, RDs, FDs, PPF and NPS kept separate
  • Kept: what's left from the month, shown on the Home screen

For students, families and everyone in between

Students use it as a pocket money tracker. Families use People & family to see money sent home or received, and groups and trips for shared spending. It works as a simple money manager on day one and grows into budgets, goals and reports when you want them.

Free, offline and private

FinInsight is free to download and works offline — it needs no internet to read messages or calculate totals. There's no login, no cloud account and no advertising ID shared. Your money diary stays on your phone, encrypted.

Questions

Is FinInsight a free money manager?

Yes. The money manager, automatic tracking, this month's and last month's reports, Scam Shield and app lock are free.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Messages are read and totals calculated on your phone, with no internet needed.

Can I keep a cash book too?

Yes — add cash spends in two taps, or type "add 250 chai cash" in the Ask bar.
