A budget tracker that keeps count for you

A budget only works if you know where you stand before the month ends. FinInsight AI tracks every payment from your bank SMS and tells you, in plain words, whether you're on track.

How to make a monthly budget in a minute

  • One overall monthly budget, or budgets per category — food, shopping, travel, anything
  • Spending fills in automatically from bank, card and UPI messages — nothing to type

How to stick to a budget: know before you overspend

FinInsight looks at your pace, not just your total. Half-way through the month it can tell you "On track for ₹46.7K — above your ₹30K budget", while there's still time to adjust. A gentle alert arrives when a category nears its limit — an overspending alert while it still helps — and the Home screen shows how much is left for the rest of the month.

Bills, EMIs and dues in the same place

  • Credit-card bills with due dates and amounts, read from your card messages
  • EMIs, insurance premiums and payment requests listed before they're due
  • Subscriptions and regular payments spotted automatically, so you can see what renews each month

Save with a goal

Create a savings goal — an emergency fund, a trip, a phone — and FinInsight counts your SIPs, RDs and account balances towards it automatically. The money score out of 100 shows how your month is going and the one thing to improve next.

Which budgeting method suits you?

Whether you want a personal, household or family budget app, any method works better when the spending side is filled in for you:

  • 50/30/20 budget — about half of take-home pay on needs, 30% on wants, 20% saved. Set category budgets to match and FinInsight shows how each part is doing.
  • Zero-based budget — give every rupee of the salary a job. The month view shows money in, spent, invested and kept, so nothing is left unaccounted.
  • Envelope budget — a budget per category works like a set of envelopes, without carrying cash.
  • Just a spending limit — one monthly number, and an alert before you cross it. A good budget for beginners.

Unlike a budget sheet or spreadsheet, there is nothing to copy across each evening.

Questions

How is this different from a budget spreadsheet?

A spreadsheet needs every payment typed in. FinInsight reads them from your bank SMS, so the budget is always up to date.

Can I set a budget for each category?

Yes — one overall budget, category budgets, or both.

Will it remind me about credit-card bills and EMIs?

Yes. Card bills, EMIs, premiums and payment requests are listed with their due dates, before they're due.

Is my budget data uploaded?

No. Everything is calculated and stored on your phone, encrypted. There is no account and no server.
