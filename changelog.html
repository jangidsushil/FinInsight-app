What's new

What's new in FinInsight

Every release, in plain words.

Version — the latest

  • What's new, after every update: the first time you open the app after updating, it shows what changed. Find it again under Settings › App updates.
  • Share a receipt: open any transaction and tap Share for a clean receipt as a picture or text, with the amount, payee, date, account, method and reference. Your balance is never on it, and the account digits can be hidden.
  • Block a scam sender properly: "Block sender" now blocks it inside FinInsight (its messages are put aside, never counted, no alerts) and takes you straight to the phone's blocked numbers with the number copied, or to its messages.
  • Mutual fund purchases are counted from the fund's confirmation in every wording we know of: from any fund house, CAMS or KFintech, or a platform such as Groww, Zerodha Coin, BSE StAR MF or NSE. The entry names the fund.
  • Coming up now includes the payments that are likely this month (rent, SIPs, subscriptions and bills you pay regularly), on Home and in the Plan. Ask answers "bills this month", "what's coming up" and "likely bills".
  • Lists of transactions open newest first everywhere, with a switch to "Largest first".

Version 2.0.64

  • Trials are real Pro now: the 7-day trial, and any extension, include every feature and show no ads. Invited by a friend? Enter their code for 14 days.
  • Scam Shield, rebuilt: it knows 28 kinds of scam in English, Hindi and Hinglish, checks every link against the bank's real website, spots look-alike and shortened links and fake senders, and says what to do — with one tap to call 1930 or report on Chakshu.
  • Check any message: paste it in Scam Shield, or share it to FinInsight from WhatsApp or your SMS app. Mark a message safe or a scam and Scam Shield learns. A weekly summary of what it caught.
  • Mutual fund purchases now count even when the bank's debit message never came, from the fund's own confirmation (duplicates, redemptions and NAV messages are left out).
  • Find a feature understands "sip calculator", "dark mode", "tax summary" and small typos, opens things inside screens, and offers to Ask when nothing matches.
  • Safe to spend today, and the month ahead: expected salary, bills due and your usual spending to month end.
  • Subscriptions with price rises, and a warning when the same charge is taken twice.
  • Card bills that don't add up: when a card's bill is more than the purchases read from its messages, the card's page shows the difference and lets you say what it was: card charges, an investment or other payment made with the card, or an older balance.
  • An emergency-fund meter in Wealth, and five new calculators: retirement, PPF, inflation, prepay or invest, rent or buy.
  • Mutual fund statements: import the CAS PDF from CAMS, KFintech or MF Central to see each fund's current value in Wealth.
  • UPI Lite payees (optional, Settings): who you paid, named from your UPI app's payment notification.
  • Family: share a password-locked summary (monthly totals and categories only) and see the household's months together. No server.
  • Hindi for the menu, tabs, Home tiles, Scam Shield and alerts (Settings › Language).
  • Insights shows a monthly accuracy check: messages read, unsure and not read, with a link to fix each.
  • Safer: alerts hide amounts on the lock screen when you use a PIN or hide amounts; repeated wrong PINs pause unlocking for a while; fingerprint unlock uses strong biometrics; updates install only when signed by FinInsight and the file matches.
  • Restoring a backup is all-or-nothing, and a backup can't change your lock or security settings.
  • If no ad is available, a small FinInsight banner holds its place; Settings › About shows the ad status. No banner on Scam Shield, search, statement checks or review.
  • Promotional messages are no longer stored.
  • Many fixes to the numbers: card-bill payments and part-paid card dues, refunds and reversals, tax surcharge and 80C, reports in Indian time, and CSV files that open safely in Excel.
  • Easier to read and tap: darker grey text and bigger touch targets.

The app tells you when a new version is out and shows what changed before you install it (Settings shows your version and checks for updates). Your books, settings and Pro stay as they are.
