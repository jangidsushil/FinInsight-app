What FinInsight can and can't see

Every permission the app uses, why it needs it, and what it never asks for.

What FinInsight can see, and why

Read and receive SMSNeeded

To read money messages from banks, cards, UPI apps, wallets and billers as they arrive and when you first open the app. Personal chats, OTPs and promotions are skipped and never stored. Scam Shield checks new messages on the phone.

NotificationsNeeded

To tell you about dues, budgets, scam messages and your money brief. You choose which alerts you get and quiet hours. When the app is locked with a PIN or amounts are hidden, alerts don't show amounts on the lock screen.

Fingerprint or face unlockOptional

For the app lock, if you turn it on. Android does the check; FinInsight never sees your fingerprint or face.

Internet and network stateNeeded

Only to load ads in the free version and to check this website for updates. Your messages and books are never sent anywhere — the build fails if the app's own code connects anywhere else.

Install appsOptional

So the in-app updater can install a new version you downloaded. Android asks you to confirm each install, and the file is checked first.

Notification accessOptional

Off unless you turn on UPI Lite payees in Settings. Reads only payment notifications from UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, CRED, Amazon Pay and a few more), only to name who a UPI Lite payment went to. Everything else is ignored at once.

Files you pickOptional

Statements, backups and family summaries are opened or saved only through Android's file picker — FinInsight sees only the file you choose.

What it can't see

Advertising ID and ad trackingRemoved

The advertising-ID, ad-topics and ad-attribution permissions are removed from the app, so they can't be read — not even by the ad SDK.

Contacts, call log, location, camera, microphoneNever asked

FinInsight doesn't ask for them and can't read them.

An account or a bank loginNever asked

There is no sign-up, no password to a bank and no FinInsight server.

Cloud backupSwitched off

Android's automatic backup is off for FinInsight. A backup exists only when you make one, locked with your own password.

What it keeps

Only money messages, and what it worked out from them — amounts, dates, accounts (by their last digits), payees and categories — encrypted inside the app. You can remove any of it, or everything, from Settings. Uninstalling removes it all.
