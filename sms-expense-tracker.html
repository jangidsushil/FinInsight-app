SMS expense tracker

Your bank already texts you every payment. FinInsight reads them for you.

Every debit, credit and card payment triggers a bank SMS. An SMS expense tracker turns those alerts into a full record of your money — and FinInsight does it without a message ever leaving your phone.

Which messages it reads

FinInsight reads transaction SMS from banks, credit cards, UPI apps, wallets, insurers, brokers and billers: debit alerts, credit alerts, card spends, bill statements, EMI and NACH debits, balance updates and payment requests. Indian commercial senders carry registered headers such as AD-HDFCBK or VM-SBIUPI, which helps it tell a real bank alert from a look-alike.

Which messages it ignores

  • OTPs are skipped and never stored
  • Personal chats from people are never read into the app
  • Promotions and offers are left out of your totals

Only money messages are kept, encrypted inside the app.

Is it safe to give SMS permission?

The SMS permission lets an app read your inbox, so it matters who you give it to. FinInsight has no server: it has nowhere to send your messages. It doesn't ask for contacts, location, microphone or camera, Android's cloud backup is turned off for it, and no advertising ID is shared. You can see the exact message behind any transaction under Why FinInsight thinks so.

Old messages, missing messages

On first open, FinInsight reads the transaction SMS already on your phone, so past months fill in straight away. If your bank skipped some alerts or you deleted messages, import the bank statement (PDF — even password-protected — or CSV). FinInsight reconciles the statement with your SMS and adds only what's missing.

Questions

Does the SMS expense tracker read OTPs?

No. OTPs are skipped and never stored.

Why does Play Protect warn about SMS apps?

Play Protect blocks apps installed from outside the Play Store that ask for SMS permission, whatever they do. Pause it for the install and switch it back on afterwards.

What if my bank doesn't send SMS for every payment?

Import a bank statement to fill the gaps. FinInsight adds only the transactions that are missing.

Are my messages uploaded?

Never. There is no FinInsight server.
