Install guide

Install FinInsight in three steps

FinInsight is installed from this website rather than the Play Store, so Android asks you to allow it first. Do these before you download.

1

Pause Play Protect

Only for this install. Google Play Protect blocks every app that reads SMS when it isn't from the Play Store — it goes by the permission, not by what the app does.

2

Allow your browser to install apps

Android calls this Install unknown apps (older phones: Unknown sources). You allow it for the browser you download with, usually Chrome.

3

Download, install, then switch Play Protect back on

Open FinInsight once, allow SMS access, and turn Play Protect on again. FinInsight keeps working, and reminds you until Play Protect is back on.

Want to check the file first? Verify it's the real FinInsight — its checksum and signing certificate are published.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my financial data uploaded anywhere?

No. FinInsight has no server and no account. Your messages are read and every insight is calculated on your phone. Your books are stored encrypted inside the app, and Android's cloud backup is switched off for it.

How is this "AI" if nothing goes to the cloud?

FinInsight's insight engine runs entirely on your phone: it understands bank messages, categorises spending, spots patterns, forecasts the month and answers questions — without sending anything to a cloud AI service.

Why do I need to pause Google Play Protect?

In India, Play Protect blocks every app that asks for SMS permission when it's installed from outside the Play Store — it judges by the permission, not by what the app does. You pause it only for the install and switch it back on straight after; FinInsight keeps working, and reminds you until it's back on.

How do I know the download is the real FinInsight?

The Verify page shows the file's SHA-256 checksum for the current version, the signing certificate every FinInsight release is signed with, and a link to the file's VirusTotal report. In-app updates check both before installing.

What can FinInsight see on my phone?

Only what it needs: your SMS (to read money messages), notifications, fingerprint unlock, and internet for ads and the update check — never for your data. Every permission, explained.

Does FinInsight read my personal chats or OTPs?

No. It looks only for bank, card, UPI, wallet and bill messages. Personal conversations and one-time passwords are skipped and never stored.

Which banks are supported?

Messages from all major Indian banks, credit cards, UPI apps and wallets — including HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak, Bank of India and many more. If a message is misread, correct it once and FinInsight remembers.

Is FinInsight free?

Yes. Tracking, this month's and last month's reports, dues, alerts, Scam Shield and app lock are free for good. The first 7 days are full Pro with no ads (14 days with a friend's invite code). After that you can open a Pro feature for an hour by watching a short ad, or buy Pro in the app. Features opened by watching an ad need an internet connection, used only to load the ad.

How many ads will I see?

None during the trial or with Pro. In the free version: a small banner, and full-screen ads only when you come back from something you finished — never in the middle of a task, never in the first hour after installing, at most 3 a visit and 6 a day, at least 4 minutes apart. Scam Shield, search, statement checks and review screens show no banner. Rewarded ads play only when you choose one.

Does Scam Shield catch every scam?

No filter can promise that, because scams change every week. In our test set of 1,528 messages (504 scams, 1,024 genuine bank, delivery and OTP messages) it caught every scam and flagged none of the genuine ones — but it was tuned on that set. Treat it as a strong second opinion: if one slips through, mark it as a scam and it learns.

Is an advertising ID or any profile shared?

No. The advertising-ID permission is removed from the app, so it can't be read or shared, and nothing from your messages or books is shared with anyone.

How do I update?

The app tells you when a new version is out and installs it from inside the app; or download again from this website and tap Update. Your books, settings and Pro stay as they are. The first time you open the new version, it shows what's new.

Can I share a receipt of a payment?

Yes. Open any transaction and tap Share: you get a clean receipt as a picture or text, with the amount, who was paid, the date, the account, the method and the reference number from the bank's message. Your balance is never on it, and you can hide the account digits.

Can FinInsight block a scam sender?

It blocks the sender inside FinInsight at once: its messages are put aside, never counted and never alert you. Stopping messages from arriving is something Android lets only the phone's own Phone and Messages apps do, so FinInsight takes you straight there with the number copied.

Does it count my SIP if the bank sends no message?

Yes. A purchase confirmation from the fund house, CAMS, KFintech, Groww, Zerodha Coin, BSE StAR MF or NSE counts as an investment even when the bank's debit message never came. If the debit arrives later, it is matched to it, so nothing is counted twice.

I got "App not installed" — what now?

You probably have a copy signed differently (for example an old test build). Make a backup in Settings › Backup, uninstall the old copy, install again and restore. If it says the package is invalid, the download was cut short — download again.
