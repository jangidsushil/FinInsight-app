Install guide

Install FinInsight in three steps

FinInsight is installed from this website rather than the Play Store, so Android asks you to allow it first. Do these before you download.

1

Pause Play Protect

Only for this install. Google Play Protect blocks every app that reads SMS when it isn't from the Play Store — it goes by the permission, not by what the app does.

2

Allow your browser to install apps

Android calls this Install unknown apps (older phones: Unknown sources). You allow it for the browser you download with, usually Chrome.

3

Download, install, then switch Play Protect back on

Open FinInsight once, allow SMS access, and turn Play Protect on again. FinInsight keeps working and reminds you.

Steps for your phone

Samsung Galaxy One UI
  1. Open the Play Store › tap your profile picture (top right) › Play Protect › ⚙ gear (top right) › turn off Scan apps with Play Protect › Turn off.
  2. Turn off Auto Blocker if your phone has it (One UI 6 and newer): Settings › Security and privacy › Auto Blocker › off. Auto Blocker stops every app from outside the Galaxy Store and Play Store.
  3. Allow Chrome to install: Settings › Apps › ⋮ (top right) › Special access › Install unknown apps › Chrome › Allow from this source. (Or wait for the prompt when you open the file and tap Settings there.)
  4. Download, open FinInsight.apk and tap Install. Then turn Auto Blocker and Play Protect back on.
Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO HyperOS / MIUI
  1. Open the Play Store › tap your profile picture (top right) › Play Protect › ⚙ gear (top right) › turn off Scan apps with Play Protect › Turn off.
  2. Allow Chrome to install: Settings › Privacy protection › Special permissions › Install unknown apps › Chrome › allow. (MIUI: Settings › Additional settings › Privacy › Special app access, or Settings › Apps › Manage apps › Chrome › Install unknown apps.)
  3. Download and open FinInsight.apk. If a security scan appears, let it finish and tap Continue installation / Install anyway. Some phones ask you to sign in to a Xiaomi account first — follow the prompt.
  4. Turn Play Protect back on after FinInsight opens.
Vivo, iQOO OriginOS / Funtouch OS
  1. Open the Play Store › tap your profile picture (top right) › Play Protect › ⚙ gear (top right) › turn off Scan apps with Play Protect › Turn off.
  2. Allow Chrome to install: Settings › Security & privacy › More security settings › Install unknown apps › Chrome › allow. (Older phones: Settings › Security › Unknown sources.)
  3. Download and open FinInsight.apk. Vivo may scan the file and ask for your vivo account password or show Install anyway — confirm to continue.
  4. Turn Play Protect back on after FinInsight opens.
Oppo, Realme, OnePlus ColorOS / realme UI / OxygenOS
  1. Open the Play Store › tap your profile picture (top right) › Play Protect › ⚙ gear (top right) › turn off Scan apps with Play Protect › Turn off.
  2. Allow Chrome to install: Settings › Apps › Special app access › Install unknown apps › Chrome › allow. (Some phones: Settings › Privacy › Install unknown apps.)
  3. Download and open FinInsight.apk. ColorOS and realme UI may show a security check or ask for your account password, and sometimes a countdown before Install anyway — wait and confirm.
  4. Turn Play Protect back on after FinInsight opens.
Motorola, Google Pixel, Nothing, Nokia and others Android as Google ships it
  1. Open the Play Store › tap your profile picture (top right) › Play Protect › ⚙ gear (top right) › turn off Scan apps with Play Protect › Turn off.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my financial data uploaded anywhere?

No. FinInsight has no server and no account. Your messages are read and every insight is calculated on your phone. Your books are stored encrypted inside the app, and Android's cloud backup is switched off for it.

How is this "AI" if nothing goes to the cloud?

FinInsight's insight engine runs entirely on your phone: it understands bank messages, categorises spending, spots patterns, forecasts the month and answers questions — without sending anything to a cloud AI service.

Why do I need to pause Google Play Protect?

In India, Play Protect blocks every app that asks for SMS permission when it's installed from outside the Play Store — it judges by the permission, not by what the app does. You pause it only for the install and switch it back on straight after; FinInsight keeps working.

Does FinInsight read my personal chats or OTPs?

No. It looks only for bank, card, UPI, wallet and bill messages. Personal conversations and one-time passwords are skipped and never stored.

Which banks are supported?

Messages from all major Indian banks, credit cards, UPI apps and wallets — including HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak, Bank of India and many more. If a message is misread, correct it once and FinInsight remembers.

Is FinInsight free?

Yes. Core tracking, reports for this month and last month, Scam Shield, alerts and app lock are free. Everything else is open during a 7-day trial and can be unlocked afterwards inside the app.

Is an advertising ID or any profile shared?

No. The advertising-ID permission is removed from the app, so it can't be read or shared, and nothing from your messages or books is shared with anyone.

How do I update?

Download again from this website and tap Update. Your books, settings and Pro stay as they are.

I got "App not installed" — what now?

You probably have a copy signed differently (for example an old test build). Make a backup in Settings › Backup, uninstall the old copy, install again and restore. If it says the package is invalid, the download was cut short — download again.
