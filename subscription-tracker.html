Recurring payments, found automatically

FinInsight looks for payments that repeat at a regular interval to the same payee: OTT and streaming services such as Netflix, Prime Video, Hotstar, Spotify and YouTube Premium; app subscriptions; gym memberships; and UPI AutoPay or card mandates. You get a list of what renews, how often and for how much.

Alerts worth having

  • New recurring payment — when a new subscription starts charging you
  • Price increase — "Now ₹649, up from about ₹499"
  • Charged twice? — the same amount to the same payee within a short time
  • Bank charges and fees — SMS charges, card fees and penalties added up

A quick subscription audit: what it costs a year

Open the Subscriptions & apps category to see every subscription payment this year. If one isn't worth it any more, cancel the unused subscription in that service's app — and FinInsight will show the saving the next month.

Good recurring payments too

Not every recurring payment is a cost to cut. Monthly SIPs and RDs are recognised as investments, and they count towards your savings goals and money score.

Questions

Can FinInsight cancel a subscription?

No — cancel it in the service's own app or website. FinInsight helps you find it.

Does it find UPI AutoPay mandates?

Yes, AutoPay debits are recognised as recurring payments from their pattern.

How does it detect a price rise?

It compares a recurring payment with what you usually paid that payee.

Is my subscription list uploaded?

No. It's worked out and stored on your phone.
