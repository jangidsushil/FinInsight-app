Credit card tracker

All your credit cards, bills and due dates in one view

With two or three cards it's easy to lose track of what's due when. FinInsight AI reads your card messages and keeps spends, statements, limits and due dates together — privately, on your phone.

Card spends, by category

Every card swipe and online card payment is recorded from the card's SMS and categorised — food, shopping, travel, fuel, bills. See spends per card or for all cards together, compare months, and export a credit card spends report.

Bills and due dates you won't miss

  • Statement amount and due date read from the card's bill message
  • Minimum due and total due shown side by side — pay the total to avoid interest
  • A reminder before the due date, and a warning if the bank balance looks short of the bill
  • Late fees and bank charges flagged, so they don't repeat

No double counting of the bill payment

When you pay a credit card bill from your bank account, the purchases have already been counted. FinInsight recognises the bill payment as a transfer, so your spending isn't doubled. Available credit limits and card balances appear under Accounts & cards.

Cashback and rewards

Credits marked as cashback or rewards are tracked as income, so you can see what your cards gave back. And if an SMS says your reward points are "expiring" with a link, Scam Shield warns you — it's a common trick to steal card details.

Questions

Which credit cards are supported?

Cards from SBI Card, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak, American Express, IDFC First, AU, RBL, OneCard, slice, Scapia and many more — see supported banks.

Will paying the card bill count as spending?

No. The bill payment is recognised and not counted again.

Does it remind me of the due date?

Yes. Card bills appear under upcoming dues with a reminder.

Does it need my card number?

No. It works from the SMS your card issuer sends. Only the last digits shown in the message are used to tell cards apart.
