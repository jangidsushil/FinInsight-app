Savings and goals

Save with a goal — and watch it fill on its own

Saving is easier when you can see progress. FinInsight AI counts your SIPs, deposits and balances towards the goals you set, and scores your month out of 100.

Download for Android All features
Free · Android 8.0 or newer · your data stays on your phone

Goal-based savings that track themselves

Create a goal — an emergency fund, a holiday, a new phone, a deposit for a home — and link it to a category, a payee or an account. Your SIPs, RDs and account balances count towards it automatically, so the progress bar moves without you entering anything.

Your investments, recognised

  • Mutual fund SIPs and lump-sum investments
  • Deposits — FDs, RDs and PPF
  • NPS and pension contributions
  • Stocks and other investments through brokers

Investments are kept separate from spending, so "how much did I save?" has a real answer.

A money score out of 100

The money score checks the habits that matter: kept part of your income, invested regularly, spent at your usual pace, stayed within budget, paid no late fees or bank charges, and sorted your spending into categories. Every point is explained, with the one thing to improve next. A saving streak shows how many days in a row you stayed on track.

A monthly savings tracker: your savings rate

See what share of your income you kept each month, and how this year compares with last year — a simple measure of financial health that no spreadsheet keeps up to date for you.

Questions

Does it show my mutual fund returns?

No. It tracks what you invest from your messages, not market value. Use your fund or broker app for returns.

Can I have several goals?

Yes — create as many goals as you like.

What is the money score?

A score out of 100 for the month, built from saving, investing, spending pace, budget, fees and categorisation, with every point explained.

Is my investment data shared?

No. It stays on your phone.
