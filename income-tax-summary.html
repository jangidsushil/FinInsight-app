Tax-year summary

Your financial year, ready for tax time

Before you file your ITR, you need the year's numbers in one place. FinInsight AI pulls them from your messages and puts the old and new tax regimes side by side.

What goes into the summary

  • Salary credits for the financial year
  • Savings and FD interest — savings interest up to ₹10,000 is deductible in the old regime (80TTA)
  • Life insurance premiums, PPF and tax-saver FDs — 80C
  • ELSS mutual fund SIPs — 80C (FinInsight lists your SIPs; check which are ELSS)
  • Health insurance premiums — 80D
  • Your own NPS contributions — an extra ₹50,000 under 80CCD(1B)
  • Rent paid — for the HRA exemption
  • Home loan EMIs — home loan interest under section 24(b), principal under 80C

Old vs new regime, side by side

FinInsight estimates tax under both regimes from your figures — deductions in the old regime, the standard deduction and employer NPS in the new — so you can see which tax regime is likely better for you this year. Every figure says which transactions it came from.

Tax documents checklist for ITR preparation

Use the summary to check your Form 16 and AIS, collect the certificates you'll need (rent receipts, the home loan interest certificate, insurance receipts), and walk into ITR season prepared — or hand it to your CA.

Any financial year, any period

Reports cover the Indian financial year (April to March) as well as calendar years, quarters and custom dates, and export to PDF or Excel for your records.

Questions

Does FinInsight file my income tax return?

No. It prepares a summary and comparison to help you or your CA file.

Is the regime comparison exact?

It's an estimate from the payments in your messages. Confirm with Form 16, AIS and your tax adviser.

Which deductions does it look for?

80C, 80D, 80CCD(1B), 80TTA savings interest, HRA rent and home loan interest under 24(b).

Is my tax data uploaded?

No. It's calculated on your phone.
